# Engine: progression and blast design

Date: 2026-07-20
Modules: `levelCurve.ts`, `blast.ts`, `fortify.ts`

Closes the last two undecided gameplay modules. Values below were chosen by
Robert on 2026-07-20; the spec froze the shape of these mechanics but never
gave numbers.

## Decisions taken

| Question          | Decision                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Level scope       | Persistent, account-level             |
| Grind to cap      | About 500 confirmed hits              |
| Weapons           | 5 tiers at 15 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 m |
| Fortify half-life | 1 hour                                |

## levelCurve

Levels run 1..11. Advancing from level L costs `round(100 * 1.3^(L-1))` XP,
satisfying the frozen "exponentially scaling XP" requirement.

Cumulative thresholds: `0, 100, 230, 399, 619, 905, 1276, 1759, 2386, 3202, 4262`.

These are committed as a constant table rather than exponentiated at runtime.
There are only ten of them, and a table is exactly reproducible in the PHP
engine without needing a fixed-point `pow`. Same reasoning as the sine table.

### The multiplier interaction

The ERD applies the reward multiplier `max(0, 1 - (level-1)/10)` to **XP as
well as coins** ("Level 11+: 0% XP/Coins"). Combined with a rising curve, the
grind is heavily back-loaded:

| Level | XP to next | Multiplier | XP per confirmed hit |  Hits |
| ----: | ---------: | ---------: | -------------------: | ----: |
|     1 |        100 |        1.0 |                   40 |   2.5 |
|     5 |        286 |        0.6 |                   24 |  11.9 |
|     8 |        627 |        0.3 |                   12 |  52.2 |
|     9 |        816 |        0.2 |                    8 | 102.0 |
|    10 |       1060 |        0.1 |                    4 | 265.0 |

Total: **499.2 confirmed hits**, of which **53% is the final level alone**.

This hits the requested target, but it is worth knowing that level 10 to 11 is
half the game. If that reads as too punishing, the single-line alternative is
to apply the multiplier to coins only and leave XP unscaled, which flattens the
curve to roughly 125 hits total. That contradicts the ERD as literally written,
so it is flagged rather than assumed.

## blast

Geometry only. `resolve` returns, per target, the distance from the impact
point and whether it fell inside the weapon's radius. A target exactly on the
radius counts as a hit.

Weapon ids are placeholders chosen for tier ordering, not content:
`scout` 15, `light` 25, `medium` 50, `heavy` 100, `siege` 200 metres. The table
is data, so renaming is a one-line change.

**Damage magnitude is deliberately not modelled.** Per-weapon damage values and
the falloff shape (flat within radius, or linear from centre) are specified
nowhere. Inventing them would be inventing balance. Open question.

The 15 m tier sits close to GPS accuracy of 5-10 m, so near-misses on a poor
fix will be contentious. Noted, not solved.

## fortify

Shields decay on a 1 hour half-life and absorb damage before base HP.

`decayFactor(elapsedMs)` computes `2^(-elapsed/halfLife)` without `Math.pow`:
the whole number of half-lives is a right shift, and the fractional remainder
comes from `decayTable.ts`, a committed 65-entry table at 1/64 resolution with
integer interpolation. Generated by `scripts/gen-decay-table.mjs`, mirroring the
sine table approach. Beyond 63 half-lives the result is zero.

`applyDamage` drains the shield first, spills the remainder onto base HP, and
floors both at zero so overkill is discarded rather than going negative.
`stack` adds a new layer onto whatever remains of the old one, which is the
frozen "stacking shield HP layer" behaviour.
